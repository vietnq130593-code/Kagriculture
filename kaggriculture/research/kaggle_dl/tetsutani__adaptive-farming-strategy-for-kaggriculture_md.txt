## 1. The game in one minute

<div class="kg-prose"><div class="lead">Kaggriculture is a <b>two-player farming economy</b>. One season is <b>30 days × 24 turns = 720 turns</b>. The player with the higher terminal bank wins. Coin margin is useful for diagnosis, but strategy promotion is decided by <b>wins, losses, and ties</b>.</div></div>

<div class="kg-strip">
  <div class="kg-mini"><b>Capital</b><small>land · animals · seeds</small></div>
  <div class="kg-mini"><b>Operations</b><small>workers · routes · timing</small></div>
  <div class="kg-mini"><b>Biology</b><small>growth · water · fertilizer</small></div>
  <div class="kg-mini"><b>Market</b><small>shared prices · town demand</small></div>
  <div class="kg-mini"><b>Finish</b><small>cash in the bank at turn 720</small></div>
</div>

<div class="kg-note gold"><div class="title">The key operating insight</div><div class="body">A high spot price can still be a bad decision if the crop takes too long, consumes scarce labor, or crashes after repeated sales. Good play is about <b>economic timing</b>, not just expensive items.</div></div>


<div class="kg-note teal"><div class="title">Current town-demand regime</div><div class="body">The Town Center now removes one of each non-fertilizer product <b>once per day at a flat rate</b>. Shops unlock <b>with replacement</b>, so duplicate shop instances can concentrate demand on a small set of products. The controller therefore reads the live shop list instead of assuming a balanced town.</div></div>

## 3. Opening economy EDA

<div class="kg-prose"><div class="lead">A high opening price is only a clue. The real decision must combine price, growth time, worker travel, feed, storage, and the price erosion caused by selling into a shared market.</div></div>

<div class="kg-insight-row">
  <div class="kg-insight"><div class="num">Insight 01</div><b>Price is stateful</b><span>A quote is not a fixed reward. Selling changes future inventory and therefore later prices.</span></div>
  <div class="kg-insight"><div class="num">Insight 02</div><b>Throughput beats headline value</b><span>A premium crop only matters if the farm can repeatedly water, harvest, transport, and sell it before the deadline.</span></div>
  <div class="kg-insight"><div class="num">Insight 03</div><b>Cash timing matters</b><span>One dollar earned early can finance labor or land; the same dollar earned on turn 719 cannot compound.</span></div>
</div>

## 4. Architecture: let early demand choose the season route

<div class="kg-prose"><div class="lead">The controller separates <b>strategic route selection</b> from <b>runtime execution repair</b>. The first few shop unlocks choose among five complete 720-turn action streams. After that, small guards repair local problems or adjust sale timing without replacing the farm plan.</div></div>

<div class="kg-flow">
  <div class="kg-flow-item"><div class="kg-flow-badge">1</div><div><b>OPENING SIGNAL</b><span>read the first 1–3 unlocked shops</span></div></div>
  <div class="kg-flow-arrow">→</div>
  <div class="kg-flow-item"><div class="kg-flow-badge">2</div><div><b>ROUTE SELECTOR</b><span>yarn-first · yarn-second · yarn-third · milk-support · generalist</span></div></div>
  <div class="kg-flow-arrow">→</div>
  <div class="kg-flow-item"><div class="kg-flow-badge">3</div><div><b>RUNTIME GUARDS</b><span>weed repair · market shift · storage · narrow counters</span></div></div>
  <div class="kg-flow-arrow">→</div>
  <div class="kg-flow-item"><div class="kg-flow-badge">4</div><div><b>DEADLINE</b><span>protect shed room · liquidate remaining value</span></div></div>
</div>

<div class="kg-note"><div class="title">Coherence still matters</div><div class="body">A dynamic market tempts the controller to chase every quote. This policy instead uses the town's opening structure to choose a full operating route, while later adaptations stay deliberately narrow. That preserves labor routing and production timing while still reacting to weeds, storage pressure, shared-market competition, and recognizable opponent shapes.</div></div>

## 6. Four practical control layers

<div class="kg-grid">
  <div class="kg-card green"><div class="eyebrow">A · Five complete season routes</div><b>Plan the whole farm, not isolated turns.</b><br><span>Each route coordinates labor, crops, livestock, logistics, expansion, and market timing across the 720-turn episode.</span></div>
  <div class="kg-card gold"><div class="eyebrow">B · Early demand selector</div><b>Use shop order as a structural signal.</b><br><span>Yarn appearing first, second, or third leads to different livestock-heavy routes. A milk-supporting shop in the first three unlocks chooses a crop-heavy route; otherwise the controller uses a generalist route.</span></div>
  <div class="kg-card violet"><div class="eyebrow">C · Execution guards</div><b>Repair local failures without inventing a second planner.</b><br><span>A weed blocking a build or plant is dug and the interrupted action is replayed. End-of-day storage pressure can redirect a worker and create room before value is lost.</span></div>
  <div class="kg-card teal"><div class="eyebrow">D · Market and closeout discipline</div><b>Respect shared-market pressure and the deadline.</b><br><span>Sell slots are ranked by estimated impact, selected near-clone situations can shift premium sales forward, recognizable opponent shapes trigger narrow counters, and the final turns liquidate sellable stock.</span></div>
</div>

<div class="kg-note gold"><div class="title">Why several routes beat one universal script</div><div class="body">The first shop unlocks are persistent demand information, not a one-turn quote. The controller therefore chooses from a small library of coherent farms instead of forcing every market regime through the same production mix.</div></div>

## 10. Mental model of one turn

<div class="kg-terminal">
live observation<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;↓<br>
read the first few unlocked shops and select the matching full-season route<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;↓<br>
read the scheduled farmer, hand, and market actions for this turn<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;↓<br>
if a weed blocks a planned build or plant, dig first and replay the local intent<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;↓<br>
if near-clone market pressure makes tomorrow's premium sale vulnerable, shift a bounded amount forward and repay the shift next turn<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;↓<br>
rank competing sell slots by estimated live market impact and town demand<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;↓<br>
apply only narrow opponent-pattern counters when the public farm shape matches<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;↓<br>
protect shed capacity near the end of each day<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;↓<br>
liquidate remaining sellable stock in the final turns<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;↓<br>
return farmer + hands + market orders
</div>

<div class="kg-note"><div class="title">Reusable design principle</div><div class="body">Separate <b>strategic commitment</b> from <b>execution repair</b>. Let a persistent signal choose the farm, then keep runtime corrections small enough that they protect—rather than erase—the route's timing and labor coordination.</div></div>
