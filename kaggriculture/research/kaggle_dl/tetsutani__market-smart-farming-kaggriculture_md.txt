## 1. Deterministic route, guarded execution
<div class="kg-prose"><div class="lead">The policy starts from a complete deterministic season route, then applies narrow guards only where a public-state resource constraint can invalidate or waste a planned action. From step 288 onward, a bounded reservation layer may move an already-scheduled sale up to four own actions earlier inside the current 72-turn shop block. Separately, input guards protect physical WHEAT and fertilizer needed by upcoming workers instead of assuming that future cash can always repair a shortage.</div><div class="micro">The guards change executable timing and resource protection; they do not use seed IDs, hidden future shops, or private opponent state.</div></div>

---

## 2. Conservative resource and market rules
<div class="kg-prose"><div class="lead">The controller keeps a two-day physical grain reserve around the early production transition, prefunds an imminent WHEAT pickup only when current orders are affordable, and refuses edits that would create new overflow or break already-funded purchases. Feed decisions charge care value only when it can affect a future production dawn. Sale reservation still stops at pickups, same-item purchases, the ten-order cap, unsupported depot states, or the next shop-block boundary.</div></div>

---

## 3. Four layers, each with a narrow job
<div class="kg-grid"><div class="kg-card green"><div class="eyebrow">Route controller</div><b>One frozen policy owns the season.</b><span>Movement, production, purchases, labor, and the baseline market plan remain coordinated.</span></div><div class="kg-card teal"><div class="eyebrow">Input protection</div><b>Physical obligations come before optional trades.</b><span>Grain and fertilizer are protected when upcoming workers need them and the current budget can fund the edit.</span></div><div class="kg-card gold"><div class="eyebrow">Market timing</div><b>Only scheduled sells may move.</b><span>The four-action reservation layer respects stock consumers, capacity, order count, and shop-block boundaries.</span></div><div class="kg-card violet"><div class="eyebrow">Closeout</div><b>Late physical delivery remains explicit.</b><span>The terminal planner handles final inventory and liquidation instead of relying on speculative lookahead.</span></div></div>
